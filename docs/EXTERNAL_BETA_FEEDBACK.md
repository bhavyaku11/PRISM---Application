## Security & Privacy Instructions

> **IMPORTANT**:
> - Never include passwords, authentication tokens, API keys, private signing keys, or database credentials in your feedback or bug reports.
> - **Privacy Reminder**: Please avoid uploading highly sensitive personal medical or financial documents during beta testing unless necessary. Prefer sample or redacted documents.
> - Uploaded policy and claim documents are processed by PRISM's secure backend services strictly to provide the application's AI grounding and document extraction functionality.
> - For more information, please review the [PRISM Privacy Policy](https://github.com/bhavyaku11/PRISM---Application/blob/main/web/app/privacy/page.tsx).
