## What Makes an Effective Bug Report?

To help the development team diagnose and resolve issues quickly, remember:

1. **Be Specific**: *"PDF upload fails on page 12 of a 5MB document with error 'Extraction timeout'"* is much more helpful than *"Upload is broken"*.
2. **Include Device Context**: Screen resolution, Android OS version, and manufacturer customizations (One UI, OxygenOS, MIUI) can impact rendering.
3. **Isolate the Steps**: Try repeating the action once to see if it happens consistently every time or only under specific conditions.
4. **Protect Your Privacy**: Redact personal identifying information (policyholder full name, PAN, bank account, medical diagnosis details) before taking screenshots.
